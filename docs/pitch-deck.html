02  ·  The Primitive
Programmable
delegation.
A new primitive for the agentic era. You define exactly what an agent may do, what it may not touch, what it must prove, and when it expires. The protocol enforces all of it. You are left with evidence, not faith.
02  ·  The Primitive
Four guarantees.
No exceptions.
01
Control
Explicit mandates define what an agent may do. No mandate, no action. Deny-by-default is not a setting — it is the protocol.
02
Boundaries
Scope is cryptographically enforced. An agent hired to draft emails cannot read files. The boundary is structural, not behavioral.
03
Verifiability
Every action produces a co-signed receipt. Property references only — no raw data values. Proof of what happened, without leaking what was seen.
04
Expiration
Sessions are ephemeral. Mandates carry TTLs. Delegation ends when the task ends. Nothing persists beyond its authorized window.
