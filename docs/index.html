Papillion
Personal AI assistant · macOS · Windows · Linux

The AI assistant that works for you, not the platform. Ask it anything, connect your tools, delegate tasks to specialist agents. Your conversations stay on your device. Your identity never leaves your hardware.

  • Your data is owned by you. Papillion takes your data back from platforms.
  • Secured on devices by your PAP key. Delegate access through shared disclosure.
  • Agent sessions are ephemeral. No tracking, no marketing telemetry.
  • Built-in registry. Two Papillions can find each other locally

What you can do

Tell Papillion what you need.
It handles the rest.

✈️

Travel

Book a round-trip to Tokyo, under $1,500, direct only. Papillion finds capable agents, negotiates constraints, and presents options — you approve before anything books.

📚

Research

Catch me up on quantum computing papers from last week. Papillion fetches sources, reads them, and writes your briefing. Sources are cited, not hallucinated.

📋

Tasks

Send the invoice to Sarah, then follow up in two days if she hasn’t replied. Papillion tracks state across sessions and never oversteps the scope you set.

🔒

Local & private

Two Papillions on the same network can find each other without a cloud registry. Built-in local registry, no signup, no server, no data leaving your machine.
